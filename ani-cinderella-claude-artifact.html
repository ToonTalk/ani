This film was made by Ani, a computer program Ken Kahn wrote at MIT in 1978. Everything Ani says about why is from Ani's own notes.

The film is drawn from a record of every frame Director filmed when this revival ran Ani's scene programs on a 550 by 413 picture: 24 frames a second, as Ken's camera shot the film, or 4, as his TV previews ran, which is the run the thesis's flipbook shows. The two are different runs, because each scene is planned from where the last one's filming left the characters. The reasons are what Ani itself recorded while planning; the plain-English readings (dotted underline) are ours, and each one opens to Ani's own words.

Making the film again runs Ani itself: the 1978 MacLisp files from the MIT tapes, on Director and a MacLisp interpreter rebuilt for this revival, here in your browser. A new story is first put into Ken's notation by a language model of your choice; everything after that is Ani's.

Pass 1, where Ani chose each character's way of moving, has its own page: Ani's Choice Points. The code itself, with every name linked to its definition: Ani's Code.
